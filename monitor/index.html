03 // WHY THIS EXISTS

BUILT FROM A
REAL MAINNET
EDGE CASE.

Arc Launch Monitor started after one newly launched Arc token appeared with live Uniswap V3 liquidity on one market-data service while another discovery platform initially interpreted the same locked position differently.

The goal is simple: give Arc builders one lightweight place to compare what public ecosystem tools currently know about a token.

V1 intentionally uses public read-only APIs and requires no wallet connection. Results are informational and may lag the underlying chain.
