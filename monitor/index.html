HEADLINE LIQUIDITY ≠ IMMEDIATELY AVAILABLE QUOTE ASSET

Concentrated-liquidity pools can be heavily one-sided. A market-data service may value the token inventory and report a larger liquidity number while the pool holds much less USDC at that moment. The USDC balance shown here is a live onchain reserve, but it is still not a guaranteed executable sell amount: the active tick range, fees, slippage and routing also matter.

06 // WHY THIS EXISTS

BUILT FROM A
REAL MAINNET
EDGE CASE.

Arc Launch Monitor started after a newly launched Arc token showed roughly $2.9K of headline liquidity on one market-data service while another platform found only about $9. Direct onchain reads showed why: the concentrated V3 pool was overwhelmingly token inventory with only about $9 of USDC held at that moment.

The goal is simple: give Arc builders one lightweight place to compare what public ecosystem tools report with what the pool contracts actually hold.

The monitor uses public read-only APIs and JSON-RPC calls. No wallet connection or transaction signing is required. Results are informational snapshots and may change immediately after a check.
