The campaign is **not** "buy before it pumps."

It is:

> Arc is live. `$EXIT` is tiny. Instead of pretending we already have a community, we are publicly documenting the first 25 real people who choose to be here.

The strongest proof points are:

- the public roster can honestly be `0 / 25`;
- holder-address count is read from Arc Explorer;

- a Founding entry requires a positive `$EXIT` balance verified through Arc RPC;
- pools, routers, project wallets and obvious duplicate-wallet attempts do not count;
- recognition stays as project history even if the member later sells;
- there is no giveaway, token reward, promised return, governance right or compensation attached to a Founding spot.

## What to measure

Do not optimize for impressions alone.

Track daily:

- verified Founding members;
- unique genuine holder-address growth;
- wallet-check attempts only if a privacy-respecting aggregate is later implemented — do not store submitted wallet addresses just for analytics;
- X profile visits;
- replies from distinct real accounts;
- campaign-page clicks;
- number of conversations that turn into genuine community participation.

The primary KPI until the roster reaches 5 should simply be:

**How many new real people chose to hold and opt in?**

## Things we will not do

- create extra wallets to increase the counter;
- recycle one person's wallets into multiple spots;
- buy our own token through fake accounts to imply demand;
- wash trade for volume;
- promise price appreciation;
- promise an airdrop or future token reward for becoming Founding 25;
- pay people to pretend they independently discovered `$EXIT`;
- display a member who did not opt in;
- claim Arc/Circle/Uniswap/ArcPad endorsement;
- hide a small or zero roster count.

The entire campaign works only if **Founding 25 means what it says**.
